# Mascot insert (Remotion + three.js), experience to 11-21

HKBU SCIE1007 课程作业的科普视频。这是片中的角色小插段（位置：S7 和 S8 之间），不是研究。台词和配音已定稿，见 `镜头设计_v3/深化/09_AI角色插段.md`；配音流程见技能 `expressive-voice`；对时规则见 `word-synced-animation`。

Paths use the environment variable `CDK20_WORK` (default `D:/cdk20_work`; never read `G:`, it may be unplugged).

## Files

- Code: `镜头设计_v2/tests/T4_remotion/src/v3/mascots/` — `entry.jsx` (composition `MascotShow2`), `Show2.jsx` (timing, mouths, cards, subtitles, camera), `Stage2.jsx` (three.js stage), `lines.json`, `data/timeline_v2.json` (generated by `build_timeline2.py`).
- Character art (Codex, transparent PNG, 1:1 from the fan reference sheets): `$CDK20_WORK/mascots/codex/v3/<claude|gpt|deepseek>/` with open, blink, wink, talk_a, talk_o, wave (plus a wink GIF). Copy to the Remotion public dir `$CDK20_WORK/mascots/pub/m/<who>/<state>.png`, plus the dialogue mix `pub/m/mix.wav`.
- Director rule: never draw the characters in SVG; only rotate these frames.

## Rules

1. **Mouth**: the speaker switches open / talk_o / talk_a from the per-frame voice envelope (thresholds 0.14 and 0.48, smoothed; a change needs the previous state held 3 frames). The others cycle open / blink / wink on different periods; `react` in `lines.json` triggers wink (0.7 s) or wave (1.3 s) right after the line; entrance wave before the first line, goodbye wave after the last.
2. **Camera**: three.js perspective, fov 30; intro dolly; speaker change = small pan (0.1 of the speaker x); `punch` lines push to z 10.25 with a short shake; the speaker steps forward and hops (0.13, punch 0.26). Characters stand at x = -3.15 / 0 / +3.15 so nobody is cropped even at the tightest push. A wider stance (3.9) cropped the side characters; the 10-frame check found it.
3. **Cards** (`CARDS` in `Show2.jsx`, from the source table in `09_AI角色插段.md`): the card for a story appears on its naming word; the number counts up and lands on the `hit` word (`hit_t` from the timeline). Each card prints its source line and a caution line (binding only; company report not peer-reviewed; single toxins; early leads).
4. **Subtitles**: burned in at the bottom, Instrument Serif (`@remotion/google-fonts/InstrumentSerif`), speaker name in the character colour. Cards stay at the top so they never overlap the subtitles. The credit line "Illustration. Characters adapted from fan designs by ZipZipPipe (Bilibili) and Shangshan Wuxing (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)." sits bottom right on a dark pill so it stays readable over the white apron.
5. **Stage**: dark theatre, hex lattice backdrop, bokeh, one beam and a colour rim glow per character, sparkles on wink. No flat gradient backdrop.
6. Audio is added in the composition with `<Audio src={staticFile("m/mix.wav")} />`; every event time comes from `timeline_v2.json`; no time is hard-coded.

## Render (1080p60, about 3 minutes for 3002 frames)

```
cd 镜头设计_v2/tests/T4_remotion
npx remotion render src/v3/mascots/entry.jsx MascotShow2 $CDK20_WORK/mascots/frames3 --sequence --image-format=jpeg --jpeg-quality=95 --gl=angle --concurrency=2 --public-dir=$CDK20_WORK/mascots/pub
<ffmpeg with libx264> -framerate 60 -i frames3/element-%04d.jpeg -i pub/m/mix.wav -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 15 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest out.mp4
```

- Rendering straight to mp4 crashed here with `write EOF` after a few dozen frames, so render a frame sequence and then encode. Remotion's bundled ffmpeg cannot write output files on this machine; use the CipherTalk ffmpeg-static build (`D:/software/tools/CipherTalk/resources/app.asar.unpacked/node_modules/ffmpeg-static/ffmpeg.exe`). TouchDesigner's ffmpeg has no libx264.
- The guard hook blocks `rm -rf` on paths under the Remotion project folder; write to a new frame folder instead of deleting.
- Deliver to `导演检查_<date>/11_AI角色插段.mp4`; keep the old version as `_v1`.

## Check list (every 10th frame, 301 frames, contact sheets of 30)

- `ffmpeg -start_number N -framerate 60 -i frames/element-%04d.jpeg -vf "select='not(mod(n,10))',scale=480:-1,tile=5x6" -frames:v 1 sheet.jpg`
- Characters never cropped (including punch-in and speaker pan); mouth follows the voice; no blank frame (only frame 0 is a deliberate cream flash for the light sweep); subtitle at the bottom and card at the top never overlap; source and credit lines readable; card number lands on its hit word; the last spoken word ends before the segment ends.
