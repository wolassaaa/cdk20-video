## 1 两个与日常生活的联系

i. Better early-stage drug discovery may create more opportunities to develop future treatments. Our case study examines this potential while distinguishing laboratory results from benefits for patients.

ii. Understanding the stages of drug discovery helps us assess news about AI-designed drugs. We can ask whether a report describes a prediction, a laboratory hit or a treatment tested in patients.

## 2 更具体的研究问题

How can AI-assisted computational methods support early-stage molecule discovery for hepatocellular carcinoma, and which benefits are supported by the CDK20 case study?

## 3 两个研究目标

i. Explain how target selection, predicted protein structures, molecule generation, computational evaluation and laboratory experiments work together in the CDK20 case.

ii. Evaluate the reported time and experimental results, and distinguish demonstrated early-stage outcomes from unmeasured efficiency gains and potential patient benefits.

## 7 主案例的问题和研究需求

The study addresses the use of predicted protein structures to support early molecule discovery when an experimental structure is not available in the study's starting context. It tests whether a predicted CDK20 structure, AI-assisted design and laboratory validation can produce experimentally confirmed hits. This does not claim that no experimental structure exists today.

Relevant search terms: CDK20, AlphaFold predicted structure, generative molecular design and experimental hit validation.

## 8 主案例的研究目标

The following objectives are reconstructed from the article's Introduction, Results and Conclusions, rather than quoted as a numbered list supplied by the authors:

- Select a disease-relevant target suitable for early small-molecule exploration.
- Use an AlphaFold-predicted CDK20 structure to guide molecule generation and computational evaluation.
- Synthesize selected molecules and identify an experimentally confirmed binding hit.
- Use follow-up design and experiments to improve binding and examine enzyme and cellular activity.
- Demonstrate the feasibility of the integrated workflow and report the time to the first binding hit.

The study does not establish a matched AI-versus-non-AI speedup or clinical benefit for patients.

## 9 两个资料评价标准

i. Check provenance and relevance: identify whether the source is an original study, review, book chapter or Perspective; verify publication information and whether it addresses our disease, method or evaluation question.

ii. Check the strength of the evidence: inspect experimental conditions, controls, repetition, uncertainty, comparison methods and conflicts of interest. Record whether we read the full text, a chapter, an abstract or only metadata. Do not treat related platform papers as independent replication.
