**6-01 · 画外 D："So, the big question. How much did the computers actually help? Let's line up before and now."**
- 怎么动："the big question" 记分板从板上立起；"computers" 顶上标题翻牌哗啦翻出；"line up" 两栏表头翻出 BEFORE 和 NOW，四行空着，每格翻牌随机抖一下待命。

**6-02 · "Seeing the lock. Before: hard lab experiments, and without a 3D shape, design was expensive, slow and difficult. Now: a free prediction, downloaded from a public database."**
- 怎么动："Seeing the lock" 行名翻出；"Before" 左凹位升起磨砂 CDK20，翻牌翻出 hard lab experiments；"expensive, slow and difficult" 三个小词依次翻出；"Now" 右凹位升起 4-02 那张 CDK20 卡片；"free prediction" 翻出；"public database" 卡片旁亮出小机柜图案。
- 怎么出来：镜头下移一行 → 6-03。

**6-03 · "Finding a hit. Before: screening real compounds by the millions, an approach a 2023 review says has hit a costly bottleneck. Now: 8,918 designs inside a computer, and just 13 molecules actually made."**
- 怎么动："by the millions" 左凹位一个透明料斗，满满的细珠往下流；"costly bottleneck" 料斗细颈处细珠堵住；"8,918 designs" 右边计数轮滚到 8,918；"just 13" 右凹位小托盘里只落下 13 颗珠子。
- 怎么出来：料斗飞到画面右侧 → 6-04。

**6-04 + 6-05 · 出镜 D、B**
- D "Millions of compounds screened before. Here? Thirteen made."：人旁边一个料斗（h23）；"Millions" 细珠流个不停；"Thirteen made" 料斗下的托盘里只剩 13 颗，排成一行。D 双手捧起的位置正好在托盘前。
- B "Thirteen. That's honestly wild."：13 颗珠子整齐跳一下。
- 遮挡：D 的手在托盘前。
- 怎么出来：13 颗珠子飞回记分板的第二行 → 6-06。

**6-06 · "Time. Before: a ten-to-seventeen-year road, with finding a hit just the opening stretch. Now: 30 days to the first hit, and a better key within another 30."**
- 怎么动："Time" 行名；"ten-to-seventeen-year road" 左凹位卷尺（h18）拉出很长，刻 10–17 years；"opening stretch" 卷尺最前面一小段变红；"30 days" 右凹位日历翻到 30；"another 30" 再翻 30。
- 怎么出来：镜头下移 → 6-07。

**6-07 · "Improving the key. Before: every change meant another costly lab round. Now: a predicted picture of the first key guided sixteen designs, and the grip got about fifteen times tighter."**
- 怎么动："costly lab round" 左凹位一枚硬币掉进一个小循环轨道，轨道转一圈；"predicted picture" 右凹位 5-07 那张 001 浮雕卡片；"sixteen designs" 卡片下扇形散开 16 把小钥匙；"fifteen times tighter" 一根小刻度尺从 1 推到约 15×。

**6-08 · "To be fair, these pairs come from different studies: they compare scale and speed, not one race run side by side. But the paper itself calls the work cost- and time-efficient."**
- 怎么动："different studies" 两栏之间一条刻出来的虚线槽亮起，旁边手写批注 different studies；"scale and speed" 右栏四格依次轻轻翻一下（不闪）；"not one race" 手写批注 not one race；"cost- and time-efficient" 记分板下方落下一块刻着这句引文的牌子，注 Ren et al. 2023。

**6-09 · 画外 B（三维板面）**
"Now imagine this keeps working further down the road. Fewer failed molecules, lower costs, fewer animal tests, less chemical waste. Computing costs electricity too. And the real prize? New treatments for patients."
- 板面上：主料杆往远处延伸，路边第 6 区的零件。
- 怎么动："further down the road" 镜头沿料杆往前推；"Fewer failed molecules" 珠子堆（p42）亮；"lower costs" 钞票亮；"fewer animal tests" 小鼠（p43）亮；"less chemical waste" 废料桶亮。这四件都是磨砂半透明的样子，表示可能有、还没量过；"electricity" 插头和闪电（p44）亮，代表代价；"New treatments for patients" 路尽头一个小人和一粒胶囊亮起。

**6-10 · 画外 B（三维板面，路）**

**6-11 · 画外 B（三维板面，路）**

- 怎么动："end of that road" 镜头滑到成品位；"walked much further" 一块金色吊牌沿料杆一路滑过去；"lirafugratinib" 吊牌停在 Approval，刻 lirafugratinib，下面小牌 approved in the US, Sep 2026；"bile duct cancer" 再补一行；"Others failed" 另一块吊牌在半路掉下浇口，变灰，刻 EXS-21546 划掉；"And 048?" 镜头回到 Cells 站的 048；"shared it" 048 吊牌上伸出几根细线连到几个白色小人。
