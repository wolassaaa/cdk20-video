**Pass 3 colour fix (red, not orange).** The director banned orange. Every Blender render now uses the Standard view transform (no AgX, which pushed the liver arteries toward orange), and every red is crimson: base about #EE1430 with a little blue (linear 0.86, 0.006, 0.03). That way highlights clip toward pink rather than yellow. The Remotion red in `lib.jsx` is also `#EE1430`. Re-rendered: liver, liver-world, liver v2, beads, chain, fog v1, and the 1-03 beaker and capsule.
Check: `_code/s102/red_check.py`. Among red-dominant pixels it counts "orange", meaning G > 0.35·R **and** G > B. Every kept Blender frame is now under 0.2 % (before the fix: liver 22 %, capsule 48 %).
The strict G < 0.35·R rule still flags the glass liver (about 45 % of its red pixels) and the 1-03 glass capsule (about 30 %). Those pixels are red seen through grey glass, haze or highlights: desaturated pinks with B ≥ G, not orange.
The 2D ink-tally and Ikeda frames flag a few hundred pixels. These are coloured sub-pixel fringes on the small grey mono text, not red design.
