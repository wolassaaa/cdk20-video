## How to read this script

- **ON-CAM** = talking head: the member at a plain wall, tripod, eye level, medium close-up. Every ON-CAM line is 19 words or fewer.
- **VO** = voice-over under visuals, spoken by the same member.
- **MUSIC-ONLY WINDOW** = no narration. The music comes up and the picture carries the peak.
- **Delivery.** Short sentences are meant to be spoken with real pauses. Let questions hang for a beat. The three crisis lines (3-09, 5-05, 8-04) should be quieter and slower.
- **Visual codes:**
  - **[CAM]** talking head;
  - **[INK]** hand-drawn ink, bone-white `#EDEBE4` on ink-black `#070707`;
  - **[TD]** TouchDesigner vessel, blood and stage flows;
  - **[3D]** molecular structure (AlphaFold DB v6, or molecules redrawn from the paper);
  - **[2D]** clean data type;
  - **ROAD MAP**: the recurring road to a medicine.
  - Accent colours: vermilion `#FF3B1F` for blood and biology, cyan `#29F0FF` for AI.
- **Labels stay honest:** "predicted model", "OUR RE-DOCKING", "schematic", "redrawn from Ren et al. 2023".
