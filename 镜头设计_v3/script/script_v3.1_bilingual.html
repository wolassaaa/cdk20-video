S3 · Background: the lock, the key, and why CDK20 · 04:26–06:26 · lead DS3 · 背景：锁、钥匙，以及为什么是 CDK20 · 04:26–06:26 · 主讲 D
3-0104:26–04:30D出镜To follow this story, picture a lock and a key.要理解这个故事，先想象一把锁和一把钥匙。
3-0204:30–04:41D画外音Proteins do much of the work in our cells. Most drugs stick tightly to one protein and change what it does, fitting into a pocket like a key in a lock.细胞里很多工作是由蛋白质完成的。大多数药物紧紧结合在某一个蛋白上，改变它的作用：药物嵌进蛋白上的一个口袋，就像钥匙插进锁里。
3-0304:41–05:00D画外音This lock-and-key idea is over a century old. The lock is called the target: the protein we want to affect. The key is a candidate molecule. A good key fits its own lock tightly and opens no others; that's called selectivity.这个“锁和钥匙”的想法已经有一百多年历史。在药物发现中，锁叫做“靶点”，也就是我们想影响的蛋白。钥匙就是“候选分子”。好钥匙要紧紧配合自己的锁，又不会打开别的锁。后面这一点叫做“选择性”。
3-0405:00–05:15D画外音Our lock is CDK20, an enzyme: a protein that does a chemical job in the cell. Its keyhole is a pocket where it normally grabs a small molecule, ATP.我们的锁是CDK20。它是一种酶，也就是在细胞里负责某项化学工作的蛋白。它的“锁孔”是一个口袋，平时用来抓住一种叫ATP的小分子。
3-0505:15–05:37D画外音Why this lock? Back to the Hong Kong team: in 52 liver tumours compared with matching non-tumour tissue, CDK20 was higher in 36. High levels went with later-stage tumours and shorter survival. And lowering CDK20 in liver cancer cells slowed their growth.为什么选这把锁？还记得那个香港团队吗？在52个与配对非肿瘤组织比较的肝肿瘤中，有36个的CDK20较高。CDK20高的病人，肿瘤往往分期更晚、生存时间更短。而在肝癌细胞中降低CDK20，细胞就长得更慢。
3-0605:37–05:45D出镜But a link is not proof. CDK20 is one possible lock, not the single cause of liver cancer.但关联不等于证明。CDK20只是一把可能的锁，不是肝癌的唯一成因。
3-0705:45–06:04B画外音In the 2023 study, an AI platform, PandaOmics, helped pick the lock. It pooled ten liver cancer data sets, over a thousand tumour samples and hundreds of healthy controls, plus research papers, to rank possible targets.在2023年的研究里，一个叫PandaOmics的AI平台帮忙挑选了这把锁。它汇总了10组肝癌数据——一千多份肿瘤样本和数百份健康对照——再加上研究文献中的证据，给可能的靶点排序。
3-0806:04–06:20B画外音People then filtered the ranking and reviewed the top 20 by hand. CDK20 came out on top: strongly linked to liver cancer, with no approved drugs against it and no lab-measured 3D structure.之后研究人员再筛选排名，人工审阅前20名。CDK20排在最前：和肝癌关联强，没有针对它的获批药物，也没有实验测定的三维结构。
3-0906:20–06:26B出镜That last point was the problem. How do you design a key without a picture of the lock?最后一点正是难题：没有锁的照片，要怎么设计钥匙？

4-0106:51–07:04B画外音A protein's shape is usually measured in hard lab experiments, and only a small fraction of known proteins have been. So the first tool is structure prediction.通常，蛋白质的形状要靠困难的实验室实验来测定，已知蛋白中只有一小部分被测过。所以第一个工具是结构预测。
4-0207:04–07:21B画外音Think of a protein as a chain of beads that folds into a 3D shape; the order of the beads is its sequence. AlphaFold learned from known structures how sequences fold, so it predicts a shape and scores its confidence in each part.把蛋白质想象成一串珠子，它会折叠成三维形状；珠子的排列顺序就是它的“序列”。AlphaFold从已知结构中学会了序列怎样折叠，所以它能预测形状，并给每个部分打一个可信度分数。
4-0307:21–07:41B画外音The team took CDK20's predicted structure from the public AlphaFold database. Most of it had high confidence, but a tail at one end had very low confidence, and in the model it blocked the keyhole: one building block sat inside the ATP pocket.团队从公开的AlphaFold数据库下载了CDK20的预测结构。大部分可信度很高，但一端的“尾巴”可信度很低，而且在模型里，它正好堵住了锁孔：尾巴上的一个组成单元就卡在ATP口袋里。
4-0407:41–07:47B出镜So the scientists cut the tail off. The computer's guess needed a human check.于是科学家把尾巴切掉了。电脑的猜测需要人来把关。
4-0507:47–07:56B画外音They kept building blocks 1 to 302. A predicted structure is a useful model, not a photograph.他们只保留第1到第302个单元。预测结构是有用的模型，但不是照片。
4-0607:56–08:14B画外音Next, imagine not searching a drawer of old keys, but cutting brand-new ones for this keyhole. That's the second tool: generative design. The team used Chemistry42, a platform running dozens of AI models side by side.接下来，想象不是在一抽屉现成的钥匙里翻找，而是为这个锁孔直接“切”出全新的钥匙。这就是第二个工具：生成式设计。团队用的是一个叫Chemistry42的平台，它同时运行几十个AI模型。
4-0708:14–08:22B画外音They gave it the pocket's shape and size, and one rule: every design must grip one spot called the hinge.他们把口袋的形状和大小告诉平台，并定了一条规则：每个设计都必须抓住一个叫“铰链”的位置。
4-0808:22–08:37B画外音The platform's filters drop designs with reactive, unstable or possibly toxic groups, and score how drug-like and easy to make each one is. These are early computer checks, not safety tests.平台的筛选器会去掉带有活泼、不稳定或可能有毒基团的设计，并给每个设计打分：像不像药、好不好合成。这些只是早期的电脑检查，不是安全性测试。

4-0908:52–09:15A画外音All this produced 8,918 new designs, far too many to make in a lab. So the team tried each key in a virtual lock. That's the third tool, docking: the computer predicts how each molecule might sit in the pocket and scores the fit. Doing this for a big batch is called virtual screening.这一切产生了8918个新设计，多到实验室根本做不完。于是团队在虚拟的锁里逐一试钥匙。这就是第三个工具——“对接”：电脑预测每个分子可能怎样待在口袋里，并给吻合程度打分。对一大批分子这样做，叫做“虚拟筛选”。
4-1009:15–09:18A出镜But a good docking score is a clue, not proof.但对接分数高只是线索，不是证明。
4-1109:18–09:32A画外音The team also grouped similar designs together, which is called clustering, so they could pick a varied set. They shortlisted 54, and chemists chose just seven to actually make.团队还把相似的设计归成一组——这叫“聚类”——以便挑出多样化的一批。他们先入围54个，再由化学家挑出7个真正合成。
4-1209:32–09:47A画外音So, for our first objective, this case used four computer tools: mining disease data to pick a target, predicting the protein's shape, generating new molecules, and docking to rank them.所以，就第一个目标而言：这个案例用了四种计算工具——挖掘疾病数据来选靶点、预测蛋白形状、生成新分子，以及用对接给分子排序。
S5 · Results: from 8,918 designs to one candidate (Objective 2 evidence) · 09:49–12:37 · lead CS5 · 结果：从 8,918 个设计到一个候选分子（目标二的证据） · 09:49–12:37 · 主讲 C
