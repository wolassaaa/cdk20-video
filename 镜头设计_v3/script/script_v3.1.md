## How to read this script

- **ON-CAM** = talking head: the member at a plain wall, tripod, eye level, medium close-up. Every ON-CAM line is 19 words or fewer, so it can be lip-synced if the voice is re-recorded.
- **VO** = voice-over under visuals, spoken by the same member.
- **MUSIC-ONLY WINDOW** = no narration. Music comes up to showcase level (about −14 LUFS short-term, per music_curation.md) and the picture carries the moment.
- Visual codes: **[CAM]** talking head · **[INK]** hand-drawn ink (bone-white `#EDEBE4` on ink-black `#070707`) · **[TD]** TouchDesigner vessel / flow visuals · **[3D]** molecular structure (AlphaFold DB v6 model, or molecules redrawn from the paper) · **[2D]** clean data type for numbers. Vermilion `#FF3B1F` = body and biology, cyan `#29F0FF` = AI and computation, at most two accents per shot.
- Numbers: speech rounds a few long numbers; the screen shows the exact value with ± spread and a short citation footnote.
- Labels stay honest: "predicted model", "OUR RE-DOCKING", "schematic", "redrawn from Ren et al. 2023" wherever that is what the viewer sees.
