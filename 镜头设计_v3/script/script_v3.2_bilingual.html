S3 · Crisis 1: nobody has seen the lock · 04:12–06:21 · lead DS3 · 危机一：没有人见过这把锁 · 04:12–06:21 · 主讲 D
3-0104:12–04:16D出镜To follow this story, picture a lock and a key.要理解这个故事，先想象一把锁和一把钥匙。
3-0204:16–04:27D画外音Proteins do much of the work in our cells. Most drugs stick tightly to one protein and change what it does, fitting into a pocket like a key in a lock.细胞里很多工作是由蛋白质完成的。大多数药物紧紧结合在某一个蛋白上，改变它的作用：药物嵌进蛋白上的一个口袋，就像钥匙插进锁里。
3-0304:27–04:39D画外音The lock is called the target. The key is a candidate molecule. A good key fits its own lock tightly and opens no others. That's called selectivity.锁叫做“靶点”。钥匙叫做“候选分子”。好钥匙要紧紧配合自己的锁，又不打开别的锁，这叫“选择性”。
3-0404:39–04:59D画外音Our lock is CDK20, an enzyme: a protein that does a chemical job in the cell. Its keyhole is a pocket where it normally grabs a small molecule, ATP. Plug that pocket with the right key, and the enzyme can't do its job.我们的锁是CDK20，一种酶，也就是在细胞里负责某项化学工作的蛋白。它的锁孔是一个口袋，平时用来抓住一种叫ATP的小分子。用对的钥匙堵住这个口袋，这个酶就没法工作了。
3-0504:59–05:20D画外音Why this lock? Back to Hong Kong. In 52 liver tumours compared with matching non-tumour tissue, CDK20 was higher in 36. High levels went with later-stage tumours and shorter survival. And lowering CDK20 in liver cancer cells slowed their growth.为什么选这把锁？回到香港：在52个与配对非肿瘤组织比较的肝肿瘤中，有36个的CDK20较高。CDK20高，往往肿瘤分期更晚、生存时间更短。而在肝癌细胞中降低CDK20，细胞就长得更慢。
3-0605:20–05:26D出镜A strong suspect. Not the single cause of liver cancer, but a lock worth trying.一个很有嫌疑的对象。它不是肝癌的唯一成因，但值得一试。
3-0705:26–05:53B画外音In the 2023 study, an AI platform, PandaOmics, helped pick it. It pooled ten liver cancer data sets, over a thousand tumour samples and hundreds of healthy controls, plus research papers. People reviewed the top 20 by hand. CDK20 came out on top: strongly linked to liver cancer, with no approved drug against it.在2023年的研究里，一个叫PandaOmics的AI平台帮忙挑中了它。它汇总了10组肝癌数据——一千多份肿瘤样本和数百份健康对照——再加上研究文献。研究人员人工审阅了前20名，CDK20排在最前：和肝癌关联强，而且还没有针对它的获批药物。
3-0805:53–06:16B画外音Then the problem. Measuring a protein's shape takes hard lab experiments, and only a small fraction of known proteins have been measured. CDK20 had no lab-measured 3D structure. And without one, even computer predictions of how a drug binds are expensive, slow and difficult.接着问题来了。测量蛋白质的形状要靠很难的实验，已知蛋白中只有一小部分被测过。CDK20没有实验测定的三维结构。而没有结构，就连用电脑预测药物怎样结合，也会昂贵、费时又困难。
